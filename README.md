# 👋 Hey, I'm Felipe — a Full-Stack Software Engineer (Front-End focused!!!) who loves what he does!

## 👨🏻‍💻 About Me

I've been on a continuous learning journey since late 2020 when I first discovered programming. Today I work as a Mid-Level Full Stack Developer, focused mainly on frontend development while being comfortable across the entire stack.

My experience spans retail, healthcare, and international projects. I enjoy building products that actually matter — from POS systems used in supermarkets to ICU management tools for hospitals.

💡 **Career highlights:**

🔹 **Duofy** *(Apr 2025 – Present)*: As the lead frontend developer of a supermarket POS system (Electron + React + Tailwind), I stabilized a product that was in a critical phase, delivered all missing features, and helped secure **2 additional pilot clients before the official launch**. I also maintain a payment integration library with Fiserv/CliSiTef (TypeScript), used across 4 POS products.

🔹 **NovoMundo.com** *(Oct 2022 – Jul 2024)*: Built APIs integrating major partners like VTEX, Serasa, and NAPP Solutions — the Serasa integration alone was projected to generate up to **BRL 100 million in gains**. Also developed automation tools that saved the commercial team more than a full workday of manual work.

🔹 **HMO Technology** *(Jan–Apr 2025 | Apr–Aug 2023)*: Built a full-stack **ICU bed management system** for Hospital Santa Casa da Misericórdia de Goiânia (Node.js + React), allowing doctors to monitor bed status in real time and families to check on hospitalized relatives. Previously worked as a Requirements Analyst on an international project ([smartmodularcanada.ca](http://smartmodularcanada.ca)), leading meetings with Canadian clients in English.

🔹 **Federal Institute of Goiás**: Led front-end development of [Olhar Seguro](https://github.com/felipealexramos), an AI-powered facial recognition access control system deployed at the IFG Inhumas Campus.
